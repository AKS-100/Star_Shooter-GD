# 🌌 Star Shooter

> **"Customize your ship, dodge the swarm, and survive the infinite cosmic frontier!"**

Blast off into the cosmic unknown in **Star Shooter**, a fast-paced, pixel-art arcade space shooter built in Unity! Dodge enemy fire, weave through asteroid fields, and take down a relentless alien fleet. Whether you are blasting your way through the handcrafted campaign or testing your reflexes in the endless survival modes, you'll need sharp skills and a quick trigger finger to save the galaxy. 

## ✨ Key Features

* 🚀 **Customizable Starfighters:** Visit the main menu to change your ship's sprite before you launch! The player's `PolygonCollider2D` dynamically updates via script to perfectly match your chosen design's hitbox.
* 🗺️ **5 Thrilling Levels & Level Select:** Progress through a 5-level campaign, and easily replay your favorite missions using the built-in Level Select UI.
* ♾️ **Infinite Survival Modes:** Test your endurance in 2 unique infinite-spawning levels using dynamic spawner managers. The enemies never stop coming!
* 👾 **Varied Enemy Fleet:** Face off against a diverse swarm of foes, from aggressive chasers to tactical straight-shooters, each driven by modular enemy scripts.
* 🔧 **Dynamic Power-Ups:** Stay in the fight by grabbing randomly spawning life-restoring power-ups (logic caps at a maximum of 3 lives to keep the game balanced).
* ❤️ **Slick UI & Visual Life Tracking:** Keep your eyes on the action with a clean, consistent pixel-art interface, featuring dynamic visual life icons and score tracking managed by a custom `UIManager`.
* 🎵 **Stellar Soundtrack:** Groove to the driving level music that keeps the energy high while you blast your way through deep space.
* 🎨 **Cohesive Pixel Art Style:** Enjoy a unified, retro-inspired 2D aesthetic that looks great and runs incredibly smooth.

## 🎮 Controls

* **Movement:** `W, A, S, D` or `Arrow Keys`
* **Aiming:** `Mouse Cursor`
* **Fire Weapon:** `Left Mouse Button`
* **Pause Game:** `Escape` 

## 💻 How to Play / Installation

**Play Online:**
You can play the game directly in your browser on itch.io here: *https://aks24550s.itch.io/star-shooter*

**Download for Windows:**
1. Go to the [Releases](../../releases) tab of this repository.
2. Download the latest `StarShooter_Windows.zip` file.
3. Extract the folder and run `Star Shooter.exe`.
